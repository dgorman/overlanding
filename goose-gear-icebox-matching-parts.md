# Building a Matching Box for the Goose Gear IceBox 1.3

**Vehicle:** Toyota 4Runner
**Goal:** Build an additional storage box that attaches to the Goose Gear IceBox 1.3 and looks identical — same silver extrusion, black corner cubes, and black textured panels.
**Why DIY:** Goose Gear will not sell raw parts. Their current frame profile is a proprietary extrusion they call **MaxLite™** (6063-T6 aluminum), which is why nothing ships as components — but every element has an off-the-shelf visual twin.

*Researched 2026-09-25 from close-up photos of the actual box (macro shots of the extrusion, tape-measure shot of the profile, corner detail).*

---

## What the IceBox is made of (identified from photos)

| Element | Identification |
|---|---|
| Frame | **1" × 1" T-slot aluminum extrusion** (measured ~1" wide), clear anodized — the "silver" finish is standard clear anodize, not paint |
| Corners | **Black 3-way squared corner cube** with counterbored thru-holes on each face (Goose Gear describes theirs as fiber-reinforced nylon; the 80/20 black-anodized aluminum part is a dead ringer) |
| Top panel | Birch-core plywood faced with **black phenolic film, hexa-grip anti-slip texture** (the dotted pattern) |
| Side panels | Same plywood, **smooth black phenolic** face — CNC-cut slots show the bare yellow ply core edges |
| Fasteners | Stainless Torx flanged button-head screws into slot T-nuts; wing-bolt eyelet tie-downs riding in the T-slot |
| Panel core note | Goose Gear's own spec is Joubert **okoume** marine ply (Lloyds-certified, ~half the weight of birch), laminated in-house with PVA cold-press. Phenolic-faced birch is the accessible look-alike; expect a few extra pounds per panel. |

| Source | Notes |
|---|---|

#### About the bevel

The MaxLite profile has a **beveled/soft-edged face** that standard 80/20 1010 (square edges, small corner radius) doesn't replicate. Options, best match first:

1. **Genuine Goose Gear extrusion via their mod kits.** They won't sell raw stock, but they DO sell the [Extrusion Frame Modification Kit x.31](https://www.goose-gear.com/products/extrusion-frame-modification-kit-x-31-for-campkitchen-icebox) (~$139) — "includes both Extrusion Bars and hardware," sized for the CampKitchen 2.3 / IceBox 1.3 footprint. Buying one or two mod kits = real MaxLite bars with the exact bevel and anodize. Call Goose Gear for bar lengths before ordering (8-week build-to-order lead time). Their [Anchor Kits](https://www.goose-gear.com/products/goose-gear-anchor-kits) cover matching tie-down hardware.
2. **TSLOTS (Futura) TS10-10QR "Quarter Round"** — 1" × 1", **two T-slots + large radius outer corner**: slots face the panels, the soft corner faces out. Much closer to the Goose Gear edge than square 1010. Sold by [Framing Tech](https://www.framingtech.com/shop/1-0x1-0-r-240inch-1-x-1-t-slot-profile-2-26-tslots-quarter-round-402620) and [Automation Werks](https://www.automationwerks.com/products/tslots-1012-s-1-in-x-1-in-quarter-round-extrusion-650040/2217522000016375453); 0.26" slots, center bore taps 1/4-20.
3. **Standard 1010 / 1010-S** — square-edged but visually close at arm's length; cheapest and most available.

Don't chamfer standard 1010 yourself — routed edges expose bare (shinier) aluminum that won't match the anodize.

### Hardware

- 1/4-20 **flanged button-head screws** (stainless Torx to match) + **10-series economy T-nuts** — TNUTZ, 8020, or Amazon 10-series kits
- Slot-mount **eyelet / wing-bolt tie-down anchors** in 10-series

## ⚠️ Measure before ordering

Two caliper checks on the existing IceBox:

1. **Profile width:** exactly **1.000"** → fractional 10-series (order above). **0.984" (25mm)** → metric 25-series instead (8020 sells that too; M6 hardware).
2. **Slot opening:** 10-series ≈ **0.26"**; 25-series ≈ 6mm. Determines which T-nuts fit.

Goose Gear builds in the USA, so fractional 10-series is the strong bet — but the check costs nothing and a wrong guess costs a return.

---

## Build approach

1. Cut 1010 to length with 1/4-20 end taps at every corner-cube joint.
2. Join the frame with 4042-Black cubes.
3. Screw phenolic panels to the frame faces: button-heads through the panel into T-nuts in the slots (exactly how the IceBox panels attach).
4. **Attaching to the IceBox:** both boxes have open T-slots — bolt them together with T-nuts and joining plates. No drilling into the Goose Gear unit.
