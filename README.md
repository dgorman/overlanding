# Overlanding (`4Runner`)

Build documentation, CAD models, and parts research for the Toyota 4Runner overlanding setup on a Goose Gear plate system.

## Projects & Documentation

- **[4Runner Electronics Box (`v12s` + `pack2`)](battery-box/README.md)** — **Active build**: the frame of a donor 25 in Goose Gear IceBox 1.3 cut down to **571.5 × 488.1 × 281.7 mm (22.5 × 19.2 × 11.1 in)**, holding a MultiPlus 12/800 (modelled to the 12/1200 envelope), two SuperPack 60Ah batteries under hold-down breaker drop shelves, and all Victron/Garmin charge and switching gear, paired with **`pack2`**, a stacked all-printed external pack of four more SuperPacks on an SB175 umbilical.
  - **[Step-by-Step Build Manual (`MANUAL.md`)](battery-box/MANUAL.md)** — Cut lists, rail tapping, print orientation table, heat-set insert map, T-nut coordinates, port cutouts, and rendered assembly diagrams
  - **[Fastener BOM & McMaster Order Pad (`mcmaster-order.md`)](battery-box/manual/mcmaster-order.md)** — Complete hardware list, direct links, and Order Pad block
  - **[Component Dimensions & STEP Files (`components.md`)](battery-box/components.md)** — Electrical/mechanical specs and 3D model sources
  - **[As-Built Audit & Slot Ledger (`audit-2026-10-30.md`)](battery-box/manual/audit-2026-10-30.md)** — Metric 25-series frame measurements and slot occupancy
  - **[Design History & Build Log (`HISTORY.md`)](battery-box/HISTORY.md)** — Chronological log and earlier reference designs (`v2`–`v13`)
- **[Goose Gear IceBox 1.3 — Matching Parts Research](goose-gear-icebox-matching-parts.md)** — Extrusion profile identification (`25.0 mm` metric 25-series MaxLite™, `25.75 mm` corner cubes, `6.0 mm` phenolic-faced okoume ply) and compatible hardware sources.
